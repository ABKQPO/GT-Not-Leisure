The radial menu in com.gtnllib.gui.wheel, the shape and frosted backdrop renderers
in com.gtnllib.client.fx.screen (FxShapes, FxFrostedBackdrop) and the shaders in
assets/gtnllib/shaders/fx are ported from the wheel module of AnvilLib
(https://github.com/Anvil-Dev/AnvilLib, branch dev/1.21.1, commit 0f817d4d).
