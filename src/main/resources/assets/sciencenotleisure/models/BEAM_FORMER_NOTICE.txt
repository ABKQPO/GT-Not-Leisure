The texture and face UV definitions are copied unchanged. The five planar base-light
overlays are offset outward by 1/1024 block to prevent depth fighting with the shell;
the remaining model geometry is unchanged. Display poses are adapted for 1.7.10.
The standalone model removes the modern vanilla block/block parent and remaps
texture and particle references to the local 1.7.10 block atlas.

JSON baking, face UV rotation, texture-pixel UV insets, orientation caching and reload handling are
adapted from the approach used by MyCTMLib. Its MIT license and copyright notice
are included in META-INF/licenses/MyCTMLib-MIT.txt.
