Used to generate computation (and heat)
Requires a continuous supply of Super Coolant to reduce heat
After forming, a series of indicators will be displayed
Different indicators provide different performance adjustments
Indicator coefficients (coolant / heat / computation):
  Horizontal: {red:Red(3 / 1.25 / 1)}, {yellow:Yellow(2 / 1.25 / 1.5)}, {green:Green(1.25 / 3 / 2)}
  Vertical: {red:Red(1.5 / 1.5 / 1.25)}, {yellow:Yellow(1.25 / 1.5 / 1.5)}, {green:Green(1.5 / 1.5 / 1.5)}
The final coefficient at each position is the product of the horizontal and vertical coefficients
Use screwdriver to toggle wireless mode
