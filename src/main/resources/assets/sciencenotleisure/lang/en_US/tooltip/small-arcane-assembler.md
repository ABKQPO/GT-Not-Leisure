Processes shaped and shapeless Arcane Workbench recipes at one recipe per operation.
Requires the machine owner to have completed the recipe research.
Place a wand, sceptre, or staff in the controller slot; crafting vis is consumed from it.
Consumes 32 EU/t; two random primal aspects gain 1-4 vis smoothly over every 128 ticks.
Each selected aspect uses 0-30 matching CV; at 30 CV it gains a 200% charge bonus and overflow transfer rises from 25% to 75%.
