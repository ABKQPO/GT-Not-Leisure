Uses dimensional technology and an indestructible drill to ruthlessly extract every drop of a planet's blood.
A more advanced infinite fluid drill.
Can extract fluids more efficiently, while requiring a certain temperature to start. Can be heated by inputting Blazing Pyrotheum.
Drill Tier: Cosmic Neutronium - 1; Infinity - 2; Transcendent Metal - 3; Space Time - 4
The drill generates heat based on its working temperature. Heat generation formula: (Drill Tier * Temperature / 2000).
As temperature increases, efficiency also increases. When temperature exceeds the critical value, the drill bit will melt.
Can input different fluids to cool down. Available coolants are as follows. Cooling consumption is fixed at 200kL / 5t.
Distilled Water 1K
Liquid Oxygen 2K
Liquid Helium 4K
Cryotheum 40K
Blazing Pyrotheum consumption formula: (Current Temperature^1.3)
Place the corresponding dimension marker block to extract fluids, supports multiple!
